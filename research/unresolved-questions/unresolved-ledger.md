# KALAKOTE OVERLOOK — UNRESOLVED RESEARCH QUESTIONS
*Honest accounting of historical questions where reliable public records are currently missing or incomplete (As of October 2026).*

In compliance with our non-negotiable research rules, the website does NOT invent missing history. Below are the specific historical gaps identified:

### Question 1: Exact Founding Date & Epigraphic Records of the Name "Kalakote"
- **Current Finding:** Local traditions claim "Kala Kot" refers to a black stone fort or outcropping black coal seams. Dogra revenue records (post-1846) list Kalakote as a settlement.
- **Unresolved:** Earliest known surviving written document using the exact name "Kalakote" has not been located prior to late 19th-century colonial gazetteers and Dogra revenue settlements.
- **Status:** RESEARCH PENDING.

### Question 2: Panjnara / Pandu Kund Patron Monarchy
- **Current Finding:** Stone shrines at Panjnara reflect Kashmiri medieval architectural canons (c. 8th-11th century CE) rarely preserved south of the Pir Panjal.
- **Unresolved:** No deciphered foundation inscription or royal charter identifies the specific Lohara or Khasa monarch who commissioned the stone kunds.
- **Status:** PARTIALLY VERIFIED.

### Question 3: Plant Salvage & Asset Liquidation of Kalakote Thermal Station
- **Current Finding:** The plant ceased generation in 1987 and was formally closed by Cabinet in 1989.
- **Unresolved:** Complete public ledger detailing whether all imported Czechoslovakian Skoda turbine components were officially auctioned as scrap or if portions remain under JKSPDC custody has not been made public.
- **Status:** RESEARCH PENDING.

### Question 4: Current Financial Ledger of Coal Mines Provident Fund (CMPF) Dues
- **Current Finding:** JKML workers and unions have repeatedly petitioned for clearance of legacy retirement dues and CMPF contributions.
- **Unresolved:** The exact contemporary 2026 audited balance sheet of unpaid legacy liabilities across retired Kalakote miners has not been released as an open public document.
- **Status:** RESEARCH PENDING.

### Question 5: Badhog Exploratory Reserve Commercial Viability
- **Current Finding:** GSI conducted core drilling in Badhog sector.
- **Unresolved:** Whether the exploratory drilling established commercially extractable seams capable of sustaining mechanized mining, or if steep Himalayan dips make commercial extraction uneconomic.
- **Status:** UNDER INVESTIGATION.
