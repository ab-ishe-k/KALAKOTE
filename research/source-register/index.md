# KALAKOTE OVERLOOK — PRIMARY & SECONDARY SOURCE REGISTER
*Last Updated: October 2026*

This register contains verified metadata for all documentary sources underpinning the digital archive. Every fact on the website links to an item in this ledger.

---

### SRC-001: JKML Official Mineral Portfolio: Coal Sector
- **Institution:** Jammu & Kashmir Minerals Limited (Government of J&K Enterprise)
- **Publication Date:** 2023-08-15
- **URL:** http://jandkminerals.in
- **Type:** Official Government Order / Public Enterprise Summary
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED
- **Coverage:** Kalakote, Metka, Moghla, Kotla coal deposits.
- **Evidence:** Confirms semi-anthracite coal rank (calorific value 6,000–7,800 Kcal/kg), low volatile (10–15%), high ash (25–45%).

### SRC-002: District Census Handbook: Rajouri (Village and Town Directory)
- **Institution:** Directorate of Census Operations, Jammu & Kashmir (Census of India 2011)
- **Publication Date:** 2014-06-01
- **Type:** Census of India
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED
- **Coverage:** Kalakote Tehsil and core settlement.
- **Evidence:** 72,667 total population, 16,243 households, sex ratio 919, area 413 sq km, literacy 59.13%. Core town ward: 558 persons.

### SRC-003: The Geology and Coal Resources of the Jammu Coalfields
- **Institution:** Geological Survey of India (GSI)
- **Publication Date:** 1989 (and ongoing Memoirs)
- **Type:** Geological Survey Memoir
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED
- **Coverage:** Kalakote, Metka, Mahogala synclinal belt.
- **Evidence:** Establishes basal Subathu Formation (Late Paleocene - Middle Eocene) unconformably overlying Great Limestone inlier. Extreme tectonic shearing causing friable character.

### SRC-004: Sanction for Upgradation of SDM Posts to ADC
- **Institution:** General Administration Department (GAD), Government of J&K (Order No. 490-GAD of 2018)
- **Publication Date:** 2018-03-23
- **Type:** Official Government Order
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED
- **Coverage:** Nowshera, Sunderbani, Kalakote subdivisions.
- **Evidence:** Conceded after 40-day agitation, upgrading SDM to independent ADC post.

### SRC-005: Ashok Kumar Sharma & Anr. v. Chander Shekhar & Anr.
- **Institution:** High Court of Jammu & Kashmir (Division Bench, LPA No. 8 of 1985 / 1991 KLJ 525)
- **Publication Date:** 1991-12-13
- **Type:** Court Judgment
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED
- **Coverage:** Power Development Department technical engineering cadre.
- **Evidence:** Sets landmark service cutoff jurisprudence involving state electrical engineering divisions.

### SRC-006: J&K Legislative Assembly Starred Question No. 112
- **Institution:** Jammu & Kashmir Legislative Assembly
- **Publication Date:** 2013-03-18
- **Type:** Assembly Proceeding
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED
- **Coverage:** Kalakote Thermal Power Station (22.5 MW, 3x7.5 MW units).
- **Evidence:** Confirms shutdown in 1987, formal closure by Cabinet in 1989, unviable coal linkage and high-ash clinkering.

### SRC-007: DGMS Safety Directives & Suspension Notices
- **Institution:** Directorate General of Mines Safety (DGMS), Northern Zone
- **Publication Date:** 2021-11-05
- **Type:** Statutory Safety Inspection Order
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED
- **Coverage:** Metka and Moghla underground incline adits.
- **Evidence:** Section 22/22A Mines Act notices mandating scientific roof support, ventilation, and cessation of unsanctioned adits.

### SRC-008: Inventory of Geological Resources of Coal in India
- **Institution:** Ministry of Coal / Coal Controller Organization
- **Publication Date:** 2024-04-01
- **Type:** National Mineral Inventory
- **Verification Status:** VERIFIED
- **Coverage:** Jammu & Kashmir Tertiary coal resources (~9.38 million tonnes).
